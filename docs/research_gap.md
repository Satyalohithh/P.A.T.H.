# Research gap

Status: frozen.

Most violence-detection systems:

1. Use *clip-level* binary fight/no-fight labels — no four-way distinction
   between normal, playful, suspicious, aggressive.
2. Drop the interpersonal structure: pairs are classified independently even
   though aggression is intrinsically dyadic.
3. Ignore proxemic context (intimate/personal/social zones).

SafeWatch AI closes these gaps with explicit **interaction windows** over
**tracked dyads**, engineered interpersonal features scaled by head height,
and a four-class behavioral taxonomy.
